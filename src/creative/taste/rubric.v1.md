# day2 creative rubric — v1

How day2's art director plans and its critic judges every post, slide and ad
it renders. Versioned: change it by adding `rubric.v2.md` (and bumping
`CURRENT` in `index.ts`), never by editing a published version — each post
stores the version it was judged with. See docs/creative-quality.md →
"Improving the taste file".

Score each criterion 0–10. A post ships when the weighted score is ≥ 7.0 and
no criterion marked **blocker** is below 6.

## Criteria

1. **Scroll-stopping first second** (weight 2) — frame 0–1 s carries a big,
   specific hook (≤ 6 words) or a striking real moment; never a logo, an empty
   background or a slow fade-in. On slides: the cover alone makes you swipe.
2. **Legibility at phone size** (weight 2, blocker) — every word readable on a
   phone held at arm's length: headlines ≥ 56 px, captions ≥ 40 px, body ≥ 30 px
   at 1080 wide; strong contrast; no text over busy photo areas without a
   backing.
3. **Safe zones** (weight 1.5, blocker) — on 9:16 nothing that must be read in
   the top 230 px, bottom 420 px or right 150 px (TikTok/Reels UI).
4. **Brand match** (weight 1.5) — the app's own colours, fonts, icon and
   sticker/illustration style; it should look like it came from the app's own
   designer, not from a template shop.
5. **Language consistency** (weight 2, blocker) — on-screen text, captions and
   the app's screens are in the audience's language. English screens under
   German copy is a fail.
6. **Real product** (weight 1.5, blocker) — the app's real screens (captures or
   store screenshots), large enough to read; no invented UI, no placeholder logo
   or letter mark.
7. **Hierarchy** (weight 1) — one idea per frame/slide: one headline, one
   supporting line, one screen, one detail. The eye knows where to go.
8. **Motion pacing** (weight 1) — something meaningful changes every 1.5–3 s;
   eased (no linear slides), cuts on beats; holds long enough to read
   (≈ 0.3 s per word).
9. **No clutter** (weight 1) — at most 3 text elements visible at once, no
   stacked badges, no decorative noise competing with the screen.
10. **Truthful & native** (weight 1) — reads like something people in this
    audience post and save; no ad-speak, no claims the app can't back.

## Good (do this)

- Hook in the first frame, set huge in the app's display font, on the brand
  colour; the real screen already rising into view.
- One step per slide/scene: number badge, ≤ 5-word headline, one sub line,
  the screen large and bleeding off the edge, the detail that matters lifted
  off it or zoomed into.
- Captions that appear word by word as they would be spoken.
- End card: the real icon, the app's name, one line, one button.
- The app's own stickers / 3D icons as accents, used sparingly (≤ 2 per frame).

## Bad (never)

- A small phone floating in the middle of a grey gradient.
- English App Store screenshots under German text (or any language mix).
- A placeholder letter instead of the icon; stock "Download now" end cards.
- Crossfades as the only motion; everything moving at once.
- Paragraphs on screen; three different text styles in one frame.
- Text under the TikTok caption / buttons area.
