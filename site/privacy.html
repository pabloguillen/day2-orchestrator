When you open a page, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, the time of the request and your browser's user agent, to deliver the page and protect it against attacks (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in a secure, working website). 3. The gora8 service

Your account

To use gora8 you sign in with GitHub, Google or a one-time email link. We store your name, email address and the workspace you belong to, so you can sign in and work with your team (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Your apps and connected accounts

You connect the services gora8 needs to work on your app, for example your code repository (GitHub), Expo, Apple App Store Connect, Google Play, Meta and TikTok. Keys and tokens are stored encrypted and are never shown again. gora8 uses them only to do what you asked for: change your code, build and release your app, read store and ad numbers, and run the campaigns you approve.

If you connect your Instagram account, Facebook Page or TikTok account on the Social page, gora8 publishes the posts it made for your app on those accounts: only posts you approved, or, if you turned on "Posts go out on their own", posts that passed its checks (TikTok posts always wait for your approval). gora8 also reads the statistics of the posts it published (for example views, likes, comments, shares and saves) to learn which posts work and to make better ones. It does not read your messages, does not post anything else and does not read data about your followers as individuals. To publish, gora8 makes each post's image or video available at an unguessable web address for up to 24 hours after the post went out. You can disconnect an account at any time on the Social page, and remove gora8's access in your Facebook, Instagram or TikTok settings.

Google user data

When you click "Sign in with Google" on the Connect your stores page, gora8 asks for access to manage Google Cloud resources and your Google Play Console. It uses this access only to set up your own Google Cloud project for your app: create a service account and its key, turn on the Android Publisher, Play Developer Reporting, Pub/Sub and Firebase APIs, set up Google Play purchase notifications, and give the service account access to your Play Console. gora8 keeps the Google sign-in for at most one hour and revokes it when the setup is finished. It does not read your email, files or other Google data, does not use Google data for advertising, and does not sell or share it.

gora8's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data about your app's users

If you add gora8's library to your app, it records how the app is used (screens, taps, crashes, purchases) so gora8 can find bugs and test improvements. Users are identified only by a random id or a hashed id, never by name or email. For this data you are the controller and gora8 processes it on your behalf (Art. 28 GDPR); we provide a data processing agreement. You are responsible for telling your users about it in your app's privacy notice; gora8 suggests the text.

Learning across apps

gora8 learns which kinds of changes work, for example "a shorter onboarding raised day-7 retention by 4%". It shares such results across customers only in an abstract form: the app's category and platform, the kind of change, the metric and the size of the effect. It never shares app names, texts, amounts, user counts or any ids, and shows a result only when at least three apps from three different accounts are behind it. You can turn this off in your account settings; turning it off also removes your existing contributions.

AI processing

gora8 uses AI models from Anthropic PBC (USA) to write code, plans and texts. Your code and the context needed for a task are sent to Anthropic for that task. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.

5. How long we keep data

We keep account data while your account exists, and delete it within 30 days after you close it, except where tax or commercial law requires us to keep records (up to 10 years for invoices). Connected keys and tokens are deleted when you disconnect a service or close your account.
